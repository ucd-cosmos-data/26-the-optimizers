First half of the first week!

### Sunday
All 22 members of Cluster 11 checked in and moved into our dorms (we're located exclusively in Hawthorn and Live Oak). We met our TI, Phoebe, and our RA, Shafiq, for the first time!

### Monday
First day of class in the lovely Physical and Data Sciences building. We had a lecture on the data science pipeline and after lunch had a lab dedicated to setting up our coding workflow. We also joined group 2 (the Optimizers) and began customizing our website! Each member wrote their own [introduction](https://ucd-cosmos-data.github.io/26-the-optimizers/members/), complete with images and some fun facts about ourselves.
