The final weekend before the ultimate project lock in...

## Friday

When entering morning lecture, we were met with a pleasant surprise: our shirts had arrived! The week before, we had designed our shirts online, making them bright green, jersey-inspired, and adorned with a palm tree sprouting coconuts with our teaching assistants' faces. Seeing them in person was amazing—unfortunately, Ashley was the only person in our class to not receive her shirt.

In the afternoon, we had our final quiz. Following that traumatic ordeal, our professors, TA, TF, and TI all gave presentations on their academic journeys. Inspired by Phoebe's presentation, we visited her favorite pho restaurant after class (SitLo Saigon!!). Friday's nightly activity was Sardines at the Death Star, where we tried not to get lost in UCD's most infamous building.

## Saturday

Cluster 11 spent the entire day competing in the COSMOS Olympics, consisting of not only sporting events but also intellectual ones. We began the day with a tug-of-war, and our team representatives were able to earn a 3rd place win. We also won 4th in the spelling bee. Though we didn't place in any of the other events, we had a lot of fun watching them play out. Other events included the multiplication bee, spaghetti marshmallow tower, paper airplane contest, sponge relay, and a cluster-wide lip sync contest!

## Sunday

The last Sunday of COSMOS 😢

Everyone enjoyed doing their own things, free from the demands of data science (if only briefly).
