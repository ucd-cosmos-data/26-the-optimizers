### Saturday

Arriving at the stands of the baseball game, we watched the Oakland A's face off against the Washington Nationals. From the start, the A's dominated the game, successfully completing run after run. We left the stands occasionally to grab refreshingly cold sorbets and other snacks, a great comfort due to the hot weather. Although we rooted for the A's, Cluster 11 applauded the Nationals when they won their first point near the end of the game. To no one's surprise, the A's celebrated a triumphant win of 15-1 against the Nationals. The Nationals' data scientist Dr. David Gagnon sat with our cluster, discussing the statistics behind the game. On the bus ride back from Sacramento to Davis, we celebrated with a traditional Norwegian rowing chant.
